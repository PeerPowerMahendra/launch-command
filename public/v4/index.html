Capabilities

An engine, not a template

Ten ad assets and a three-email sequence per brief — each one written for the platform it ships to, not resized into it.

Audience intelligence, not autocomplete

Before writing a word of copy, the engine drafts the strategy: who’s buying, what triggered them, where they hang out, and the one positioning line the brand says against the status quo. Every ad draws from it — and you can leave the audience field blank; it infers who has the problem.

Char-limit-aware copy

Meta primary text, Google’s 30-character headlines, TikTok captions — every line is written inside its platform’s limits, never truncated after the fact.

RSA asset builder

A complete Responsive Search Ad in one pass: ten headlines and four descriptions, mixed for coverage so Google’s combinator always has something to say.

TikTok hook engineering

Three scripts per brief, each built around a three-second open loop — the question, the reveal, the reason to stop scrolling before the swipe.

One-click launch

Approve the tabs and press launch. Meta lands in your real ad account, paused for your review — nothing spends until you flip it on. Google and TikTok are simulated, for now.

Live launch analytics

Impressions, clicks, and spend tick upward per platform the moment you launch. Watch which channel wins without leaving the console.

FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers

Is the launch real?

Meta is real today — hit launch and the campaign is created in your actual ad account, paused, waiting for your review. Nothing spends until you flip it on. Google and TikTok are simulated for now: placements go live in sequence and the analytics tick with modelled numbers while we finish those integrations.

Which engine writes the copy?

Your choice of two. The local engine drives Claude Code on your own machine — no API key, no per-token cost. The API engine calls the Anthropic API directly for faster, unattended generation. Both produce the same structure: persona, positioning, platform copy, and emails.

Can I edit the outputs?

Every field. Each platform tab is inline-editable — click any headline, description, or script line and rewrite it. Character counters keep you inside platform limits while you type, and nothing launches until you approve it.

Where does my data live?

On your disk. Campaigns are stored as local JSON files in a single-user setup — there is no shared cloud database, no account system, and nothing leaves your machine unless you choose the API engine, which sends only your brief to the Anthropic API to generate copy.

What platforms are supported?

Meta Ads (3 ad variants), Google Ads (one full RSA: 10 headlines and 4 descriptions), TikTok Ads (3 hook-first scripts), and a 3-email nurture sequence — 10 ad assets plus 3 emails from every brief, all in one voice.

What does it cost to run?

The Solo tier is genuinely $0 — the local engine uses your existing Claude Code session, so there’s no metered bill. On the API engine, a full campaign generation typically costs a few cents of Anthropic API usage, which is yours to control with your own key.
