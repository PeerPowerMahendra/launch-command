Privacy Policy

Effective July 26, 2026 · Launch Command (pre-launch software)

What Launch Command is

Launch Command is a single-user campaign workspace. It generates marketing copy from a product brief you write, and can create advertising campaigns in your own Meta ad account on your instruction.

What data we collect

  • Your product briefs and generated campaigns are stored as files on the computer running the app — not in any cloud database operated by us.
  • We do not collect, sell, or share personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics trackers, and no advertising identifiers on this site.

How the Meta integration works

  • The app connects to the Meta Marketing API using an access token that you provide, stored only on your own machine.
  • It creates campaigns, ad sets, and ads only in your own ad account, only when you press Launch, and always in a paused state — nothing runs or spends until you activate it yourself in Meta Ads Manager.
  • The app reads only what it needs to do this: your ad account's name, currency, and status, and the objects it creates.
  • No Meta user data is stored beyond the access token you supply, and none is shared with any third party.

Generation engines

When you generate a campaign, your product brief is processed by the AI engine you configured (a local Claude Code installation, or the Anthropic API with your own key). Briefs are not sent anywhere else.

Data deletion

All app data lives in the data/ folder of your installation — delete those files (or the whole folder) to remove everything. To revoke the app's Meta access, remove it at facebook.com/settings → Business integrations, or reset the token in your Meta developer app dashboard. To request deletion of anything else, contact the operator of this installation.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the site operator via the email associated with this deployment.
